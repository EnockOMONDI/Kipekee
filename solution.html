Our solution

Kipekee handles the work. Your team approves.

From the first inquiry to the final follow-up, Kipekee turns scattered travel requests into organized workflows for quotes, visas, suppliers, groups, and client communication.

Real workflows

Six operating scenarios.

Each workflow is designed for the daily work that slows down travel agencies: inquiries, groups, visas, suppliers, sales follow-up, and marketing.

01

Inquiry to Quote Engine

Email received, details extracted, itinerary and quote drafted, human approves, Kipekee sends and tracks the response.

02

Travel Coordinator

Passenger lists, rooming, visa checklists, communication, and trip movement are organized from one workflow.

03

Visa Desk Assistant

Documents are checked, missing items are flagged, reminders are drafted, and submission waits for approval.

04

Hotel & Supplier Sourcing

Hotels and suppliers are identified, compared, and packaged into RFQs your team can approve before sending.

05

Sales Follow-Up Engine

Inactive leads are detected, follow-ups are drafted, and client conversations keep moving without manual chasing.

06

Marketing Content Engine

Campaign content is generated, reviewed by a human, published, and tracked for engagement.
